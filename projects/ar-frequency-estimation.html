DIGITAL SIGNAL PROCESSING / MATLAB

AR Frequency Estimation of Voice Signal

A MATLAB-based digital signal processing project applying autoregressive modeling techniques to a recorded voice signal and estimating its dominant frequency components.

MATLAB DSP AR Modeling Burg Yule-Walker Levinson-Durbin

PROJECT AREA

Digital Signal Processing

PLATFORM

MATLAB

INPUT

Recorded Voice Signal

OUTPUT

Dominant Frequency Estimates

01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

The objective of this project was to analyze a recorded voice signal using autoregressive signal modeling and estimate the dominant frequency components represented by the AR model.

Three related AR approaches were implemented and compared: Burg, Yule-Walker and Levinson-Durbin.

03 / METHODS

AR Estimation Methods

Burg Method

Applied autoregressive modeling using the Burg approach to estimate model coefficients from the recorded signal.

Yule-Walker

Estimated AR parameters using the Yule-Walker equations derived from signal autocorrelation.

Levinson-Durbin

Applied Levinson-Durbin recursion in the AR coefficient estimation and analysis process.
