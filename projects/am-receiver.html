COMMUNICATION ENGINEERING / ELECTRONICS

Design & Implementation of an AM Receiver

An open-ended communication engineering project involving the design, construction and evaluation of a functional amplitude-modulation receiver.

AM Communication Receiver Design Hardware Tuning Noise Reduction

COURSE

EEE 3208 — Communication Theory Lab

PROJECT TYPE

Open-Ended Lab

CORE SYSTEM

AM Receiver

TARGET

Bangladesh Betar AM Reception

01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

The project was designed as an open-ended implementation of an AM communication receiver rather than a fixed predefined laboratory circuit.

The work required applying theoretical AM communication concepts to practical receiver hardware, testing, troubleshooting and performance evaluation.

02 / OBJECTIVES

Main Objectives

  • Apply theoretical AM communication concepts to practical hardware.
  • Develop hands-on experience in receiver design, construction and testing.
  • Investigate real-world reception issues such as noise.
  • Explore simulation or analysis tools where appropriate.
  • Develop technical problem-solving and collaborative engineering skills.

03 / PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

Receiver Requirements

Receiver Circuit

Design and implement a functional AM receiver circuit using an appropriate receiver architecture.

Station Reception

Enable reception of AM broadcasts, particularly Bangladesh Betar stations relevant to the Dhaka region.

Tuning Mechanism

Provide a practical tuning mechanism for selecting the desired AM broadcast station.

Noise Cancellation

Investigate and demonstrate a method for improving reception quality in the presence of noise.

04 / ENGINEERING WORK

Technical Areas Involved

  • AM modulation and demodulation concepts
  • Receiver circuit design
  • Frequency tuning and station selection
  • Electronic component selection
  • Hardware implementation and troubleshooting
  • Noise mitigation
  • Performance testing and evaluation

05 / PERFORMANCE

Intended Performance Evaluation

The project specification required practical evaluation of receiver performance and encouraged measurements such as sensitivity, selectivity and signal-to-noise ratio.

  • Ability to receive AM broadcast stations
  • Quality and stability of tuning
  • Functionality of receiver stages
  • Noise-reduction effectiveness
  • Overall reception quality

Portfolio note: The project file currently available is the original project specification. Final circuit values, measured performance and implementation-specific results can be added here once the completed project report is available.

06 / LEARNING

Engineering Skills Developed

  • Communication-system hardware implementation
  • Circuit design and testing
  • AM receiver principles
  • Tuning and frequency selection
  • Noise-related troubleshooting
  • Collaborative engineering development
